<ChangelogEntry version="Kit v0.4" date="14/10/2026">Navegação, layout e feedback: Checkbox, Toggle, Chip, Segmented Control, Filter Item, Accordion, Breadcrumb, menu mobile, Bottom Nav, Header com nova busca, Footer com Newsletter, drawers e Toast.</ChangelogEntry>
<ChangelogEntry version="Kit v0.3" date="14/10/2026">Nova linguagem visual (clean, inspirada no shadcn); botões só com Default, Hover e Disabled; Inputs e Select refeitos; raio padrão 10px; Grocery Button e Cart Button com variáveis de protótipo.</ChangelogEntry>
<ChangelogEntry version="Kit v0.2" date="13/10/2026">Componentes de e-commerce: Button, Icon Button, Tag, Price Block, Quantity Selector, Product Card vertical e horizontal, Logo, Input e Select.</ChangelogEntry>
<ChangelogEntry version="Kit v0.1" date="13/10/2026">Foundations: collections Brand, Color, Shape, Dimension e Responsive; presets de raio; tipografia, sombras e alturas de controle.</ChangelogEntry>
